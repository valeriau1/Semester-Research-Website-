MANIFESTOWhy this exists
We have a habit of becoming interested in things.

Not casually.

The kind of interested that ends with fifteen open tabs, three books ordered, an entirely new vocabulary, and no clear memory of how the question started.

So we gave it rules.
01

One subject every month.

We independently research the same broad topic.

02

No previews.

Neither person sees the other's interpretation before presentation night.

03

Presentation night.

At the end of the month, we teach each other what we found.

04

One secret inquiry each.

Every semester, both researchers choose one hidden long-form subject.

05

Clues are mandatory.

The secret subject must leave traces inside the monthly work.

06

Final defense.

At semester's end, we guess the other person's inquiry before both dossiers are declassified.
